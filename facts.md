## Main panels

1. **Kerch Bridge struck (Russia/Ukraine).** Ukraine said one of the Kerch Strait bridge's steel arches and the road span beneath it were destroyed in a drone and missile strike. Zelenskyy called it an "unprecedented operation". Russia said drones hit the bridge on Monday and Tuesday. Peskov said the bridge would be restored "even better than it was before". The railway line was still standing, but its arch was damaged.

2. **Iran–US strikes in the Gulf.** The IRGC said its drones targeted a US radar at Sheikh Isa Air Base in Bahrain. Bahrain said it intercepted them, and CENTCOM reported no damage. This was the third Iranian attempt on Bahrain in under a week. Hours later, US jets struck a missile launch site on Iran's Qeshm Island, and no casualties were reported.
